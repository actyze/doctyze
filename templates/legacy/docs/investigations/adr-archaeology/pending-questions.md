# Pending senior-engineer interview questions

> **Time-bounded.** These questions exist because Doctyze could NOT extract
> the answers from the code alone. They require a human who was there when
> the decisions were made. If your senior engineers are within 24 months of
> retirement, this is urgent.
>
> Generated by Doctyze. Each question maps to a 🔴 GAP marker somewhere
> in the documentation. Once answered, the corresponding artifact moves
> from 🔴 GAP to 🟢 CONFIRMED.

## How to use this file

1. Schedule a 1-hour interview with the most senior engineer(s) who
   worked on this system.
2. Walk through these questions in order. Don't try to do it all in one
   session — schedule a follow-up if needed.
3. Capture answers as numbered `INTERVIEW-NNN-<topic>.md` files in
   [`./`](.) (this directory).
4. For each question answered, move the corresponding artifact from
   🔴 GAP to 🟢 CONFIRMED in the relevant doc.

## Question bank

### Architectural choices

- **Why was {{LEGACY_LANGUAGE}} chosen** for this system? What were the
  alternatives considered? Any specific incident or constraint that drove
  the choice?
- **Why this data platform** ({{DATA_PLATFORM}})? When was it chosen, and
  by whom?
- **Are there any "load-bearing decisions"** that are not visible in code —
  e.g., "we never use feature X because of an incident in YYYY"?

### Operational lore

- **Which programs are "do not touch" without coordinating** with a specific
  operator or owner team? Why?
- **What's the worst production incident** you remember in this system?
  What caused it, what fixed it, and what implicit rule did everyone
  internalize afterward?
- **Are there scheduled events** (month-end close, year-end, peak seasons)
  that have special operational treatment? What's the playbook?

### Interface quirks

- **Which inbound interfaces** have downstream consumers that depend on
  undocumented behavior? (E.g., a specific field-position bug that
  consumers now expect.)
- **Which outbound interfaces** require special care because the receiver
  is fragile, third-party, or owned by another team?

### People & ownership

- **Who are the other senior engineers** who know this system? Who is
  retiring soon? Who is staying long-term?
- **What knowledge would be lost** if you (the person being interviewed)
  left tomorrow? Be specific.

### Modernization context

- **Has modernization been attempted before?** What happened?
- **What would you change** about this system if you could start over?
- **What's the "right" target architecture** in your view, even if it's
  not the current plan?
